ABOUT US

Technology, opportunity and hope.

The Kathangali Digital Skills Project was created to give children access to practical computer and digital skills, especially in a community where many families cannot afford such opportunities.

The program is offered free to children and has been tested through community and school settings. The project is hosted by EAPC Mailu and has reached children from Mailu, Kimerei and neighbouring areas.

WHAT WE TEACH

Learning skills for tomorrow.

💻 Computer Basics

Building confidence with computers and essential digital skills.

🌐 Web Development

Introducing children to HTML and the foundations of creating websites.

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Helping children understand and creatively use emerging AI technologies.

🎨 Animation & Design

Developing creativity through animation, digital design and visual storytelling.

📚 Book Making

Teaching children how digital tools can be used to create educational books.

🚀 Future Skills

Preparing learners for app development and other income-generating digital opportunities.

OUR JOURNEY

From computer basics to future digital creators.

PHASE 1

Computer Basics

Children begin with fundamental computer skills and digital confidence.

PHASE 2

Creative Digital Skills

Book making, T-shirt design/printing and other practical creative skills.

PHASE 3

Advanced Digital Skills

Animation, AI and app development as the project grows.
