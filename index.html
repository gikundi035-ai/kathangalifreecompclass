ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology, opportunity and hope.

The Kathangali Digital Skills Project was created to give children access to practical computer and digital skills, especially in a community where many families cannot afford such opportunities.

🌱 A Free Community Initiative

The program is offered free to children and has been tested through community and school settings.

🤝 Community Based

The project is hosted by EAPC Mailu and has reached children from Mailu, Kimerei and neighbouring areas.

WHAT WE TEACH

Skills for the digital future.
