ABOUT US

Technology, opportunity and hope.

The Kathangali Digital Skills Project was created to give children access to practical computer and digital skills, especially in a community where many families cannot afford such opportunities.

The program is offered free to children and has been tested through community and school settings. The project is hosted by EAPC Mailu and has reached children from Mailu, Kimerei and neighbouring areas.

About the Kathangali Digital Skills Project

The Kathangali Digital Skills Project is a free community-based initiative designed to help children gain practical computer and digital skills that can open opportunities for their future.

The project begins with computer basics and progresses into web development, artificial intelligence, animation, digital design, book making and other practical digital skills. The goal is to help children become confident digital creators rather than only consumers of technology.

The program has been tested through community and school settings and is hosted by EAPC Mailu. It has reached children from Mailu, Kimerei and neighbouring areas.

As the project grows, we are seeking support for computers, internet/Wi-Fi, printing equipment, learning materials, books, T-shirts, furniture and other resources that can help us reach more children.
